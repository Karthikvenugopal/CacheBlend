Scope and input caveats are fixed in `intervention-map.md`. Cases: airline_cancel and bank_withdraw only, using the exact aligned IDs already reproduced. Bank is a mechanically useful but semantically qualified padded-amount intervention; do not count its intended-label recovery as an unqualified ordinary-document factual result.

## Frozen support family and strict bound

The final prompt token is always a fresh readout, outside the repair budget. The changed envelope [16,19) is mandatory. Additional budgets are **16 and 32 tokens**, approximately 5% and 10% of the prompt. Count mandatory edit/readout/decode work separately.

Partition [edit.end, final_token) into complete 16-token blocks anchored at edit.end. If there is a partial tail, add one end-aligned complete 16-token block so every suffix token belongs to the candidate family. Enumerate **every single block** and **every unordered pair of disjoint blocks**. Overlapping pairs are excluded to keep exactly matched unique-token budgets. Local-16 is the first block [19,35); local-32 is the first two [19,51). Both are contained in the enumeration. No answer-dependent support or tie-breaking choice enters this plan.

Airline: 19 regions, 19 singletons + 171 pairs = 190 choices. Bank: 20 regions, 20 singletons + 189 disjoint pairs = 209 choices. **399 planned oracle support attempts combined, hard ceiling 400.** No extra supports after seeing outcomes. Baseline/validation controls are limited to five per case: full recomputation, published unrepaired intervention, actual edit-only recomputation, all-position endpoint and full-suffix endpoint (at most ten control/gate trials). Original-cache prefill, model loading, geometry and equality checks are setup and have their own costs. Random-model functional checks are not factual search trials.

This is exhaustive only over this fixed 16-token region family at one/two regions, using one all-layer repair schedule. It is not an upper bound over arbitrary tokens, windows, layers, dependency paths, repair operators, or all possible selective methods. The headroom maximum is label-identified afterward and is not a practical selector.

## Execution, output and costs

Native Qwen3 computes selected embeddings, Q/K normalization, absolute RoPE, causal attention, residuals and MLPs layer by layer. A narrowly scoped indexed-cache adapter writes selected K/V at their original positions and returns the full old/fresh causal cache. Every attempt clones the original cache; no clean downstream states are transplanted into the repair. The clean cache is permitted only for published-baseline reconstruction and scoring/endpoint checks. Final-token readout and generation branch independently and leave the repaired/base cache unchanged.

Keep every control and attempted support, including errors and incomplete attempts, generated IDs/text, truncation, repaired positions, next-token max logit difference, relative L2, KL/TV against full, top-token/output agreement and measured costs. Record cache cloning, sparse forward, diagnostic logit readout, generation including its independent cache copy, serialization and end-to-end trial wall time. The diagnostic and generation each replay the final token once; charge both rather than hiding duplicate measurement work. Geometry/enumeration and source/baseline preparation costs are separate; no attention metadata collection is needed. Record total process wall time and process/device memory samples where available. Counts of recomputed token-layers are not runtime or speedup evidence.

## Evaluation after all fixed attempts

Do not score known answer labels until enumeration finishes (or a mandatory stop preserves an incomplete run). Record generic logit/full-output agreement during execution; it cannot guide allocation or early stopping. Primary utility is the complete sole decision word, ignoring capitalization and terminal `.?!`: airline `deny` versus `refund`; bank intended `decline` versus `approve`. Other responses are inconclusive unless a separately retained manual semantic audit clearly explains them; no post-hoc promotion may change the primary counts. Any truncation is inconclusive. Full-output agreement is a separate fidelity measure. Treat bank factual semantics as qualified throughout.

At each matched budget, report all successful supports, local outcome and whether **any** oracle support succeeds where local fails. Retain ties and failures. Use token/text coordinates and singleton-versus-pair comparisons to describe dependencies; these are intervention evidence, not a proof of a unique causal circuit. No held-out/generalization claim. If useful repair is observed, propose at most one operator/mechanism hypothesis grounded in the pattern and identify prior overlap; do not implement it or a selector. If not, report the bounded negative result without expanding the search. Stop after reporting.
